# AI Growth Suite — Social Media Content, Performance & Outreach Dashboard

An AI-powered social media growth platform for modern brands. It helps content teams plan, create, repurpose, approve, and distribute on-brand content across multiple platforms — while also managing creator outreach and tracking performance.

## Purpose

The system exists to solve a core problem for growth-focused brands: **creating consistent, on-brand content at scale without losing quality control.** It brings the entire content lifecycle into one place — from ideation to approval to distribution — with AI doing the heavy lifting and humans staying in control of brand safety.

## What it does

### Content creation
- **Content Generator** — Turn a brief into a ready-to-post caption with hook, CTA, hashtags, and visual suggestion. Every output is checked against brand-safety rules.
- **Video Scripts** — Generate structured short-form video scripts with hook, beat-by-beat breakdowns, B-roll suggestions, on-screen text, and platform recommendations.
- **Image Studio** — Generate brand-aligned image concepts with brand-fit scoring and approval routing.

### Content repurposing
- **Multi-Platform Repurposing** — Take one piece of content and automatically adapt it for Instagram, TikTok, LinkedIn, X, and Facebook — each with its own hook, copy, CTA, and visual suggestion tuned to the platform.

### Brand control
- **Brand Reference Library** — Store brand guidelines, voice notes, approved language, and visual references that the AI uses when generating content.
- **Knowledge Memory** — A markdown-based knowledge base that feeds all AI outputs. Add campaign priorities, approved messaging, claims to avoid, and audience notes — the AI reads them before generating anything.
- **Approval Queue** — Nothing goes out without human review. Every AI-generated item — content, scripts, images, repurposed posts, outreach messages — flows into a central approval queue where team members can approve, reject, schedule, or send.

### Performance tracking
- **Dashboard** — KPI overview, engagement charts, content and outreach pipelines, and weekly AI recommendations.
- **Performance / Analytics** — Filterable post-level performance data with AI-generated insights and recommended actions per post. Track engagement over time and break down performance by theme, platform, and hook.

### Creator outreach
- **Creator Search** — Find creators by category and platform with fit reasoning, suggested collaboration angles, and outreach actions.
- **Outreach Drafts** — AI-personalized outreach messages with personalization details pulled from each creator's profile.
- **Sending Status** — Track the status of every outreach message — scheduled, sent, replied — in one view.

### Settings
- **Brand Voice** — Define tone, personality, reading level, CTA style, words to use and avoid, and approved claims.
- **Content Guardrails** — Set rules for what the AI can and cannot say.
- **Dropdown Values** — Manage all dropdown options across the app dynamically — content goals, platforms, tones, categories, and more — without touching code.
- **API Connections** — Configure AI providers, image generation, creator data, email, and social platform integrations.
- **User Roles** — Admin, Reviewer, Content Creator, and Outreach Manager roles with different permissions.

## How it works

1. **Set up your brand** — Add your brand voice, guardrails, and knowledge base entries in Settings and Knowledge Memory.
2. **Generate content** — Use the Content Generator, Video Scripts, or Image Studio to create content. The AI reads your brand context and knowledge base before generating.
3. **Repurpose across platforms** — Take any generated content and adapt it for every platform in one click.
4. **Review and approve** — Everything flows into the Approval Queue. Review, approve, reject, schedule, or send.
5. **Track performance** — Monitor how published content performs and get AI recommendations for improvement.
6. **Find and outreach creators** — Search for creators, generate personalized outreach messages, and track sending status.
